# film.mov

Movies and TV shows on your phone, tablet and Android TV. On a TV everything is driven by the remote, on a phone by touch. No ads, no sign-up. Needs Android 7.0 or newer.

## What it does

- **TV and phone on one core.** A remote-first interface for Android TV boxes (NVIDIA Shield, Google TV and similar) and a touch interface for phones and tablets, with the same catalog, history and settings.
- **Sources.** In English mode the app starts with Movy (English catalog, original audio up to 2160p), torrents and GE.Movie. Several Russian sites can be turned on in Settings → Sources.
- **Torrents that start in seconds.** A built-in torrent engine: pick a film, choose the quality, and it plays within seconds on a healthy torrent. Original audio comes first; English subtitles switch on by themselves when the audio isn't English (from the file, the site or OpenSubtitles). Magnet links and .torrent files open straight in the app. Uploading to other peers is off by default in English mode.
- **Sound and picture.** Dolby Digital, DTS and TrueHD are decoded by the app's own FFmpeg, so no receiver is needed; passthrough to a receiver is a setting. Dolby Vision on NVIDIA Shield. Frame zoom for films with black bars baked in.
- **Subtitles.** From the stream, from the site or from OpenSubtitles; online subtitles are aligned to the speech automatically.
- **Continue anywhere.** History and "Continue" are shared between your TV and phone over the home network; a film can be opened on the other device with one tap. Casting to a TV from the phone.
- **Voice search** on the TV remote.
- **Language.** English or Russian, asked at first start and switchable in Settings.

film.mov does not host any content; it plays what public sites and torrent trackers provide.
